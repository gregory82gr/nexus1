## Owned

- `Worker.cs` was removed from both hosts, not kept alongside the new
  `Program.cs` — it was scaffold-time `dotnet new worker` boilerplate that
  only logged a timestamp every second; it never composed anything real,
  so there was nothing worth preserving (ADR-007).
- Both hosts are composition roots only: DI registration for their
  composed contexts' Application/Infrastructure layers plus the two health
  endpoints. No business logic — matching the dependency law
  `Nexus1.ArchitectureTests` already enforces (Host layer has no
  structural restriction because none was ever needed here: neither
  `Program.cs` file contains anything beyond registration and endpoint
  mapping).
- `IIdGenerator`/`IDateTimeProvider` (`Nexus1.BuildingBlocks.Application
  .AddBuildingBlocksApplication()`) are registered once per host, shared
  across every composed context in that host — not once per context.
- Not verified in this environment: `Nexus1.RootCause.Host` actually
  running as a genuinely separate deployment from `Nexus1.ModularRuntime`
  (both were run as local processes on this one machine; real
  independent-deployment behavior — different machines, different
  lifecycles — is a claim about the broker step and beyond, not something
  this step's evidence can speak to). No RabbitMQ wiring exists yet (§5
  step 7) — `EvaluateReadingCommand`'s ReactorFleet→AlarmManagement path
  and any AlarmFloodDetected/RootCauseVerdictIssued publication remain
  in-process/untriggered from either host's composition root.
