Availability

NO SOURCE

Ch. 6 marked this figure NO SOURCE twenty chapters ago and it stays that way here: an availability figure is a property of a retained history (online time ÷ elapsed time over a stated window), and no such history exists in this backend to compute it from. Checked directly: no unit online/offline transition is ever recorded anywhere (ReactorFleet.Unit has no status field at all, only Code/Name), and no scram/trip event is ever recorded with both a timestamp and an actor — the closest match, a "Trip" alarm severity, is automatically raised with no actor field. This is a total absence of the retention mechanism itself, not a thin history — so this screen does not show "insufficient history" (that would imply a mechanism exists), and it does not show a computed percentage of any kind.

Had the mechanism existed, the computation would have been: online time ÷ elapsed time over an explicit window (e.g. trailing 30 days), reported only with its window and its transition count, and marked UNCALIBRATED if the retained history were simulated rather than real plant history — never a bare percentage. None of that runs today; described here so the gap is precise, not vague.
